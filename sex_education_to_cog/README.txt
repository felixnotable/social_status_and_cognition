In this group, we research the association between sex and education. 
Data are selected based on the following ruel: 
Starting age at the earliest available wave was 65–67
Valid Cog data were present in every wave from 8 through 13

What value does the research add?

All participants begin at a tightly controlled age of 65–67.
The 2,703 participants have complete Cog observations across waves 8–13, covering approximately ten years.
The sample is more racially and ethnically diverse than cohorts such as Rancho Bernardo.
All participants are observed across the same six historical waves, reducing variation in follow-up timing and age at entry.

We want to answer questions like: Among U.S. adults entering older age at 65–67 and followed over ten years, does the association between education and cognitive trajectory differ by sex?

Primary latent-cognition model

Outcome:
  Cog_T3 (continuous latent cognition; higher = better)

Main exposure:
  Equal-weight overall contact change = T2 composite - T1 composite

Baseline exposure:
  Equal-weight T1 contact composite

Baseline cognition adjustment:
  Cog_T2

Core covariates:
  age_T2 centered at 75
  sex_T2 (Female reference)
  education_T2 (years)
  race_ethnicity_T2 (White, non-Hispanic reference)
  asinh(wealth_T2 / 100000)
  marital status grouped to:
      Married/partnered (reference)
      Separated/divorced
      Widowed
      Never married
  cohort (A reference)

Estimator:
  OLS with HC3 robust standard errors

Samples:
  Relationship-specific complete-six T1/T2 contact sample,
  then complete-case T2/T3 latent cognition and core covariates.

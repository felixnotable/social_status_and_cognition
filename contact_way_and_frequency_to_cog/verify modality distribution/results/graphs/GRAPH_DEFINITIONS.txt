Graph definitions
1) valid_frequency_by_relationship.png
   Count = respondents with has_group_raw == 1 and at least one nonmissing modality score.

2) modality_percentage_<relationship>.png
   Denominator = valid_frequency_<relationship> from graph 1.
   Numerator = respondents with nonmissing value in the named modality.

3) analysis_score_lines_<relationship>_<modality>.png
   Each line is one analysis score (0..5). Percentages come from analysis_score_distributions.csv.

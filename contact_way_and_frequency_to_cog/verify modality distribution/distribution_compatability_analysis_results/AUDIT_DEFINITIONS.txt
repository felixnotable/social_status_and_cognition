Audit sample definition

For each relationship domain separately, include only participants with all six
derived contact-frequency scores nonmissing:
  T1 in-person
  T1 phone
  T1 written/email
  T2 in-person
  T2 phone
  T2 written/email

No cognition or covariate completeness is required for this audit.

Graph 1:
  valid_frequency_by_relationship.png
  Count = complete-six longitudinal contact sample for that relationship.

Graph 2:
  modality_percentage_<relationship>.png
  All three modality lines equal 100% by construction because every participant
  has every modality at both T1 and T2.

Graph 3:
  analysis_score_lines_<relationship>_<modality>.png
  Each line is one analysis score (0..5).
  Percentages use the same fixed complete-six denominator for that relationship
  and cohort.
